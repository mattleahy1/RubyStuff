GAME MODE

HOW TO PLAY

Shoot the invaders before they land. Each time the fleet hits the edge of the screen it drops one row. The fewer invaders left, the faster they march.

SCORING

  • Squid 30 · Crab 20 · Octopus 10
  • Mystery ship 50-300. Count your shots for the big one.
  • Bonus ship at 1,500 points

GAME MODES

  • Classic: the 1978 rules.
  • Plus: power-ups, combos and wave bonuses.
  • Hardcore: one life, fast bombs.
  • Time Attack: 2 minutes. Dying costs 10 seconds.
  • Daily: the same game for everyone today.
  • Practice: invaders never shoot.

POWER-UPS (PLUS, TIME, DAILY)

  • Catch the falling capsules. The mystery ship always drops one.
  • R rapid fire · S spread shot · L laser · T time warp · H shield repair · + extra life
  • Hit 8 invaders in a row without missing to build a combo, up to x4.

CONTROLS

  • Buttons: hold the arrows to move, hold FIRE to shoot. Slide your thumb between arrows. You can also tap the playfield to fire.
  • Drag: the cannon follows your finger and fires by itself.
  • Trackpad: swipe anywhere to nudge the cannon.
  • Tilt: tilt your phone to steer.
  • Keyboard: arrows or A/D, SPACE fires, P pauses, M mutes
  • Game controllers work in game and in menus

TIPS

  • Shields soak up shots from both sides. Shoot a gap to fire through.
  • You can shoot down enemy bombs.
  • A red line means the invaders are about to land.
  • No sound? Check your phone's silent switch.
